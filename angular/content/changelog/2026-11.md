title: VirtualScroll, and autofilled fields that keep their look
---

Changes only the Angular port has.

## New

- [VirtualScroll](/docs/virtual-scroll): Renders only the rows and columns in view, plus a buffer, so a 10,000-row table or a time matrix with hundreds of columns stays light. Nest an `axis: 'x'` window inside the rows to virtualize both axes; inside an `ff-table` it keeps the table's semantics and its fluid hover. (#15)

## Updated

- [InputGroup](/docs/input-group): A field the browser autofills keeps its own look, with no second square box inside the rounded field, at rest, focused and through a theme change. (#13)
- **Icon packs**: Every icon is its own export, named for its slot, such as `phosphorCheck` from `@angel-r/fluid-functionalism-angular/icon-packs/phosphor`, so an app that swaps a few slots bundles those glyphs alone instead of the whole pack (9 to 46 KB minified). The pack maps such as `phosphorIcons` still work. (6e1baf4)
